[01] Payment exceptions

Which open dollars are real misses, and which retry would charge someone twice?

I reconcile insurance, patient cards, ACH, checks, and refunds. The miss is rarely “the claim is open.” It is a trace posted twice, a card marked failed after the webhook settled, cash on the wrong patient, or a short pay with no denial.

What the code does

seed.py builds one synthetic dental week and the SQLite ledger. investigate.py prints the exception queue. reprocess_plan.py stays a dry run until --execute.

A patient card or ACH retry is planned only when that idempotency key has never been posted. An insurance short pay is an appeal, not a card retry. --execute writes a plan row. It does not create a payment.

The week

159 claims. 140 fully reconciled and off the queue.

BreakAmount
Duplicate ERA or card posts$225
Card marked failed after the webhook settled$82
Decline posted by hand$96
Cash on the wrong patient$155
Insurance short pay with no denial$90
Refund larger than the payment$25

A blind retry of attempts whose key is already posted would resend $258. The safe reprocess plan is $193 across 2 attempts.

How to run

From the repo root:

python3 payment-exceptions/python/seed.py
python3 payment-exceptions/python/investigate.py
python3 payment-exceptions/python/reprocess_plan.py

Python 3 standard library only. The figures above are the synthetic week, not a live ledger.
